Machine Learning · AI Odyssey 2026

ner-pk — Pakistani-News Named Entity Recognition

Fine-tuned XLM-RoBERTa-large to find 20 entity types in Pakistani news: people, government bodies, political parties, laws, CNIC numbers, money and more. Training data came from two LLM labellers with agreement filtering plus synthetic pattern sentences, and a two-stage curriculum. A regex rules layer and document-level consistency pass sit on top, shipped as a CPU-ready CLI.
