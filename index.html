Selected projects

Competition winnerMachine Learning · Oct 2026

ner-pk: named entity recognition for Pakistani news

A fine-tuned XLM-RoBERTa-large model that finds 20 kinds of entities in Pakistani news text: people, government bodies, political parties, laws, CNIC numbers, money and more.

Training data came from two LLM labellers with agreement filtering, plus synthetic pattern sentences, trained in two stages. A regex rules layer and a document-level consistency pass sit on top, and it ships as a CPU-ready command-line tool that reads text, JSONL or CoNLL.

PyTorch / Transformers / LLM labelling / regex / pytest
